# Investor demo checkpoints

## Available after releasing Phase 1

1. Open Pocket Broker and search Varna.
2. Filter by neighbourhood; unknown quarters remain visibly unconfirmed.
3. Open an offer and see the original agency link and same-city sibling prices.
4. Switch to Sofia: it is selectable with an honest empty-market message.
5. Reload: the city preference remains. Agencies and counts follow that city.

This checkpoint is validated on a restored DB; the live service has not been
switched to V3. Do not present old inactive Sofia records as current inventory.

## Full expansion demo — later phases

Verified multi-agency Sofia search → property/source/price history → favorite →
Favorites → agency's anonymous interest dashboard → explicit viewing request to
one named agency → consented lead in that agency's inbox → logged status update.
Accounts, favorites, partner analytics and leads must be implemented and tested
before this complete flow is advertised. No fabricated leads or market metrics.
